Privacy Policy

Your Data Safety is Our Priority

At GEOID STUDY CENTRE, we are committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining transparency about how your data is collected and used.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal details such as your name, email, contact information, and academic records when you enroll or interact with our website to provide a better learning experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to manage admissions, provide regular updates, and improve our services. Your data is strictly used for educational purposes and is never sold to third parties.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies to optimize user experience and analyze site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

4. Data Security

We apply advanced security protocols and encryption to protect your data from unauthorized access, ensuring that your personal details remain confidential.

5. Policy Updates

Our privacy policy may be revised periodically to stay updated with new regulations. Any changes will be posted on this page immediately.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, please reach us at geoidstudycentre@gmail.com.
