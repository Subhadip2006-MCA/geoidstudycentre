Return & Refund Policy

At GEOID STUDY CENTRE, we provide digital educational services with transparency. Please read our return and refund terms carefully before purchasing.

1. Digital Courses: Once a course is purchased and accessed, refunds cannot be issued as digital content is non-returnable.

2. Duplicate Payment: If a duplicate transaction occurs, inform us within 7 days. After verification, a refund will be processed to your original payment method.

3. Technical Issues: If you cannot access a course due to system error on our side, we will resolve it or provide an alternative solution.

4. Subscription Cancellation: Subscription can be cancelled anytime, but refunds are not allowed for the current cycle.

5. Physical Materials (If Provided): Damaged or incorrect study materials may be replaced within 7 working days upon valid proof.

For refund-related queries, contact: support@eduvision.in
